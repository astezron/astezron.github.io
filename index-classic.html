About

Research

Supervised classification of regionalized data

Doctoral thesis · Combining geostatistics and machine learning

Geological variables are regionalized: nearby samples are correlated, and ignoring that structure leaves information on the table. My thesis develops workflows that feed spatially aware features — kriging proxies, simulation-based features, and geostatistical filtering — into machine learning classifiers (primarily XGBoost) to map hydrothermal alteration types in porphyry Cu–Au deposits.

Related interests include spectral (co)simulation, the linear model of coregionalization, cost-sensitive learning (MetaCost) and multiclass boosting.
