Research

Supervised classification of regionalized data

Geological variables are regionalized — nearby samples are correlated, and ignoring that structure leaves information on the table. My thesis feeds spatially aware features (kriging proxies, simulation-based features, geostatistical filtering) into machine-learning classifiers, primarily XGBoost, to map hydrothermal alteration types in porphyry Cu–Au deposits.

Related interests: spectral (co)simulation, the linear model of coregionalization, cost-sensitive learning (MetaCost) and multiclass boosting.
